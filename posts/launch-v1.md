For every FinOps practitioner who's been asked *"why does this cloud bill matter to the business?"* — I built something this weekend.

**The FinOps Value Map.**

It's a visual model that connects technology consumption to business value, layer by layer. From GMV at the top, all the way down to the GPU running your AI ranking model.

Two ways to read it:
→ **Forward:** *"What value is gained by using this tech?"*
→ **Reverse:** *"What's at risk if this tech is removed or degrades?"*

It's agnostic — adapt it to your own org.

v1 ships with a marketplace example: 37 components across 6 layers. Click any node, see the full chain of value and dependency light up.

🔗 Try it: https://finops-value-map.vercel.app

Next week — v2: adding **waste, efficiency, and RoI quantification** on top of the map. So you can see not just where value flows, but where it leaks.
